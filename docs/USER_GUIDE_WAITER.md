# Waiter Guide — Cafe Audit System

Floor waiters use the **Waiter Tablet** to take table orders, review bills, and verify mobile payments.

Demo login: `waiter1` / `admin123`

## Waiter tablet (manual ordering)

When a customer cannot use the QR menu, or you need to add items for a table:

1. Go to **Waiter Tablet** in the sidebar
2. Tap the table number
3. Add products to the cart and submit to kitchen
4. The kitchen display shows the order immediately

## Active tables & billing

- **Active Tables** lists every occupied table and open orders
- When a customer taps **Pay Bill**, you can review payment screenshots and mark orders paid
- When a customer taps **Request Waiter** (no order yet), your tablet shows an alert — accept and go to the table

## Shifts

Waiters are **auto clocked-in on login** and clocked-out on logout. You must be on an open shift to receive customer waiter requests.
